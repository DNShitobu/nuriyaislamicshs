Administration built to support learning, welfare, and accountability

The school's administrative structure helps families understand who handles admissions, academics, discipline, welfare, and routine office support.

How the school is organized

Nuriya Islamic Senior High School is led through a coordinated structure that connects vision, daily operations, academic standards, and student welfare.

  • The proprietor and board provide strategic direction and institutional oversight.
  • The headship team coordinates academics, discipline, staff supervision, and school routines.
  • House and welfare offices support the daily well-being of boarding and day students.

When to contact the office

Parents and applicants should contact the school for admissions guidance, reporting concerns, fee clarification, timetable questions, and student support matters.

Administrative units

Each unit plays a specific role in keeping the school safe, responsive, and academically focused.

Headship

Provides overall coordination, policy implementation, school discipline, and strategic direction for teaching and learning.

Academic Office

Oversees timetables, internal assessment planning, teacher monitoring, and programme delivery.

Admissions Desk

Guides applicants through eligibility checks, documentation, placement communication, and reporting instructions.

Accounts Office

Handles fee schedules, payment guidance, receipts, and financial clarification for families.

Guidance and Welfare

Supports student conduct, adjustment, mentoring, and referrals where additional care is needed.

House Administration

Supervises boarding routines, prep, cleanliness, safety, and communication with parents on welfare matters.
