Home / Library

A library culture that supports reading, revision, and independent study

The school library is designed to give students a quiet, supervised place to read, revise, research, and build the independent learning habits needed for examination success.

What the library provides

The library is more than a room of books. It is a structured study environment that supports both academic performance and reading culture.

  • Core textbooks and revision references for major subject areas
  • Guided access to digital research and ICT-supported study sessions
  • Quiet reading periods and support for exam preparation

Opening guide

Session Availability
Weekday mornings Before classes for quiet reading and textbook access
Class hours Scheduled visits under teacher supervision
Afternoons and prep time Revision, assignment support, and focused study

Library services

Students are encouraged to use the library regularly as part of their academic routine rather than only during exams.

Reference support

Students can consult textbooks, dictionaries, atlases, and subject revision materials for independent learning.

Study supervision

The library offers a quieter alternative to group spaces when students need concentrated reading and revision time.

Reading development

Students are encouraged to build stronger reading habits beyond compulsory schoolwork through guided reading culture initiatives.

Borrowing and use rules

Rule Expectation
Book care All items must be handled carefully and returned in good condition
Return period Borrowed materials should be returned by the agreed date
Study conduct Noise should be kept low to protect concentration for all users
ICT access Any digital use should be supervised and directly related to school work

Best ways to benefit from the library

Students who use the library well usually improve both focus and confidence.

  • Visit weekly for reading and revision rather than waiting until exams
  • Use the library to compare multiple sources when preparing assignments
  • Ask subject teachers for recommended texts linked to your programme
