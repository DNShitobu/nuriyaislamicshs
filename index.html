Principal's Message

Welcome to Nuriya Islamic Senior High School

Nuriya Islamic Senior High School was established with a vision to provide quality education integrated with Islamic values. Our mission is to nurture academically excellent, morally upright, and spiritually grounded individuals who will become leaders in their communities and beyond.

At Nuriya Islamic SHS, we believe in the harmony of religious knowledge and modern education. Our dedicated staff works tirelessly to ensure that every student receives the best possible education while strengthening their faith and character.

Read More

Our Vision

To become a center of excellence in Islamic education, producing academically competent and morally upright citizens who contribute meaningfully to national development.

Our Mission

To provide quality secondary education rooted in Islamic principles, fostering intellectual growth, spiritual development, and character building.
