A calm, values-led environment for serious learning

Our school community is built around worship, study discipline, mutual respect, and practical preparation for tertiary education and community leadership.

  • Daily routines balance classroom work, guided study, and spiritual reflection.
  • Teachers, house staff, and parents work together to support student progress.

From the principal's office

Students thrive when they are challenged academically and supported personally. Nuriya Islamic Senior High School exists to provide both.

We expect our learners to be disciplined in study, honest in conduct, respectful in speech, and committed to service. The school programme is designed to build character as deliberately as it builds examination readiness.

Vision, mission, and values

Every part of the school programme is shaped by a simple conviction: academic excellence and moral formation should reinforce each other.

Our vision

To be a respected Islamic senior high school known for disciplined scholarship, ethical leadership, and strong preparation for higher education.

Our mission

To deliver quality secondary education grounded in faith, strong teaching, sound guidance, and a culture of responsibility.

Our values

  • Reverence, discipline, and sincerity
  • Academic excellence with integrity
  • Respectful community life
  • Service-oriented leadership
