Admissions made clearer, step by step

Use this page as your application guide. It takes you from document preparation to submission, follow-up, and final reporting instructions.

Use the official Nuriya admissions portal to check portal status and complete the online application when admissions are open. Open Admission Portal

Application steps

Applicants who move through these stages carefully are less likely to face delays or missing-document issues.

1

Check the requirements

Review the entry requirements, programme options, and the list of documents you must provide.

2

Prepare all documents

Gather your results, photographs where needed, guardian contacts, and any transfer-related records before starting the form.

3

Complete the application form accurately

Fill in names, programme preferences, contact details, and academic records carefully to prevent avoidable follow-up corrections.

4

Submit and confirm receipt

Return the application through the approved school channel and confirm that all supporting items have been received.

5

Watch for communication from the school

Successful applicants should monitor updates on placement, reporting dates, fee guidance, and what to bring on reopening day.

Before you submit

A final check helps applicants avoid the most common admissions mistakes.

  • Confirm the programme choice is clearly indicated
  • Make sure parent or guardian contact details are accurate
  • Check that every required document is attached or available
  • Ask questions early if any part of the process is unclear

After you submit

The process does not end at submission. Families should stay reachable and ready for the next stage.

  • Keep your phone and email active for school communication
  • Review the fees page so you understand expected charges and timing
  • Watch for reporting and orientation dates in the academic calendar
