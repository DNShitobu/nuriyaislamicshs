Home / Leadership Messages

Leadership voices focused on standards, care, and student growth

The school's leadership team works from a shared belief: students do best when expectations are clear, support is dependable, and values are lived consistently.

PM

Message from the principal

Nuriya Islamic Senior High School exists to raise learners who are serious about knowledge, grounded in faith, and prepared to serve society responsibly.

  • High expectations in both conduct and academics
  • Close collaboration between staff, students, and parents
  • Practical preparation for tertiary study and adulthood

Principal's charge to students and families

The principal's office sets the tone for discipline, academic seriousness, and the kind of support systems students should expect on campus.

We want every student to understand that growth requires both opportunity and effort. The school provides teaching, supervision, mentoring, and structure. Students are expected to respond with discipline, honesty, and a willingness to improve continuously.

Families are partners in this work. When home and school expectations align, students mature faster, learn more deeply, and become more dependable in character. That is the kind of graduate Nuriya aims to produce.

Messages from the wider leadership team

Each leadership office contributes to the school experience in a different way, but all share the same commitment to faith, order, and student progress.

PR

Proprietor

Vision stewardship and institutional growth

The proprietor's focus is to preserve the founding purpose of the school while ensuring that staffing, systems, and long-term development remain aligned with that purpose.

School identity Growth planning Community trust
AH

Assistant Headmaster

Academic coordination and daily school flow

This office helps to maintain classroom standards, teacher coordination, internal supervision, and a school day that runs in an orderly and academically productive way.

Instruction Monitoring Student follow-up
SH

Senior Housemaster

Boarding discipline and welfare for boys

Boarding life must be safe, orderly, and supportive. The house office helps students build routine, self-management, and respect for shared living standards.

Boarding routine Mentoring Parent communication
SM

Senior Housemistress

Pastoral care and welfare for girls

The girls' welfare office emphasizes safety, dignity, confidence, and the support needed for students to remain focused and healthy throughout the term.

Well-being Guidance Support systems

Leadership principle one

Discipline is treated as a framework for growth, not simply punishment. Clear routines help students focus and mature.

Leadership principle two

Academic performance is monitored closely so that support can be offered early, before small struggles become bigger problems.

Leadership principle three

Parents deserve timely, respectful communication because the strongest student outcomes are produced through partnership.
