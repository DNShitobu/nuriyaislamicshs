Provisional 2026/2027 academic calendar for planning and communication

This calendar helps students and families plan around term dates, reporting windows, revision periods, examinations, and major school activities.

This calendar is presented as a planning guide. Official circulars from the school should be treated as final where updates become necessary.

Term structure

Term Dates Key academic focus
First Term September 10, 2026 to December 15, 2026 Orientation, early assessments, parent briefing, end-of-term exams
Second Term January 10, 2027 to April 14, 2027 Continuous assessment, revision blocks, inter-house activities, examinations
Third Term May 2, 2027 to July 28, 2027 Mock preparation, project completion, final reporting, closing activities

Important checkpoints during the year

These highlights give families a practical picture of what each term is expected to include.

Beginning of term

Registration, orientation, settling into class routines, timetable confirmation, and welfare checks for returning and new students.

Mid-term period

Internal assessments, progress monitoring, club activity reviews, and parent communication for students needing added support.

End-of-term period

Revision, examinations, report preparation, dormitory clearance, and school closing procedures.

Community and student life events

Activity Expected window
Orientation for new students Opening week of first term
Parent engagement and progress meetings Mid-term and end-of-term periods
Inter-house activities and sports Second term
Mock examinations for final-year students Third term

How families can use this page

A school calendar becomes useful when it is tied to real planning and follow-up.

  • Use reporting windows to plan transport and fee discussions early
  • Check exam periods before making travel or family event commitments
  • Ask the academic office about any changes affecting your child's programme
