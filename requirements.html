Admissions start with a clear understanding of who can apply and what to prepare

This page helps prospective students and families understand the standard entry routes, expected documentation, and the preparation needed for successful admission.

Who may apply

Nuriya welcomes applicants who are ready to meet academic expectations and participate in the values and routines of the school community.

BECE candidates

Students seeking direct SHS admission should present valid BECE results and indicate programme preference where required.

Transfer students

Students transferring from another senior high school may be considered subject to available space, academic records, and conduct reports.

Boarding and day applicants

Applicants should indicate whether they seek boarding or day placement so the school can guide them appropriately.

Required documents

Preparing documents early makes the application process faster and reduces avoidable delays.

  • BECE result slip or equivalent academic record
  • Completed admission form with accurate applicant details
  • Passport-size photographs where required by the school
  • Parent or guardian contact information
  • Transfer letter and previous school report, where applicable

What the school looks for

Requirement area Why it matters
Academic readiness Shows whether the student can cope with the chosen programme
Conduct and discipline Supports smooth adjustment into the school's culture and routines
Complete documentation Allows the school to process admission decisions efficiently
Programme fit Helps the student enter a pathway aligned with strengths and goals
