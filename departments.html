Departments that combine subject mastery with guided student support

Each department is responsible not only for teaching its subjects, but also for mentoring students, planning assessments, and supporting long-term academic growth.

Academic departments

These departments shape the teaching experience across all programmes and help students build both competence and confidence.

Islamic and Arabic Studies

Builds religious understanding, Arabic literacy, moral reflection, and the values framework that supports school culture.

Science

Supports practical reasoning, experimentation, and laboratory thinking across biology, chemistry, and physics.

Languages

Strengthens communication, reading, writing, and public speaking through English and related language studies.

Humanities and Social Studies

Develops civic understanding, social awareness, analysis, and appreciation of history, geography, and society.

Mathematics

Reinforces reasoning, problem solving, and the quantitative foundation needed across all programme pathways.

Business Education

Introduces accounting, business management, and commercial awareness for students interested in enterprise and finance.

ICT and Media

Builds practical digital literacy, responsible technology use, and tools for research, presentation, and collaboration.

Visual Arts

Encourages technical skill, observation, creativity, and disciplined craft in drawing, design, and visual communication.

What departments are expected to deliver

Departments do more than assign lessons. They help maintain a strong and consistent academic environment across the school.

  • Scheme of work planning and term assessment coordination
  • Feedback on student progress and remedial support
  • Subject clubs, practical activities, and competition preparation
  • Guidance on programme selection and tertiary pathways

How departments support learners

Students learn best when academic expectations are matched by accessible guidance and regular follow-up.

Study clinics Exam revision Project guidance Club mentorship Career direction Practical work
