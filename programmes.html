Programme pathways designed around aptitude, interest, and future direction

Students choose from a range of programmes that prepare them for university, professional training, entrepreneurship, and meaningful service to society.

Programme options

Each programme combines core SHS subjects with electives that open distinct academic and career pathways.

General Science

Ideal for students pursuing medicine, nursing, engineering, environmental science, or laboratory-based careers.

Biology Chemistry Physics Elective Math

General Arts

Prepares students for law, journalism, public administration, political science, teaching, and other humanities pathways.

History Geography Literature Government

Business

Supports students interested in accounting, management, entrepreneurship, banking, and commerce-related studies.

Accounting Economics Business Management Costing

Visual Arts

Develops artistic technique, design thinking, and practical portfolio skills for creative and technical professions.

Graphic Design Picture Making Textiles Ceramics

Home Economics

Helps students build knowledge in nutrition, family living, clothing, and hospitality-related practical skills.

Food and Nutrition Management in Living Clothing and Textiles

Agricultural Science

Introduces students to crop science, animal production, and agricultural entrepreneurship with practical relevance.

Crop Science Animal Husbandry General Agriculture

How to choose wisely

Programme choice should reflect both present strengths and future ambition. A strong choice considers more than popularity.

  • Review your strongest BECE subjects and learning habits
  • Consider the courses and careers you may want after SHS
  • Ask for guidance from parents, teachers, and the admissions office
  • Choose a path you can commit to with discipline and consistency

All programmes include core SHS subjects

Regardless of pathway, every student studies the shared core subjects expected at senior high school level.

Core area Purpose
English Language Academic communication, comprehension, writing, and speaking skills
Core Mathematics Numeracy, logical reasoning, and foundational quantitative skills
Integrated Science Scientific literacy and practical understanding of the natural world
Social Studies Citizenship, critical thinking, and social awareness
