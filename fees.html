Fee guidance that explains what families should expect and when

The exact approved amount for each intake is communicated by the school. This page explains the major fee categories, payment stages, and who to contact for clarification.

Fee figures are issued with admission communication and may vary by programme type, boarding status, and approved school charges. Families should always rely on the latest school notice for exact payment amounts.

Main fee categories

Fee item When it is paid What it covers
Admission processing During application or confirmation stage Application handling and onboarding preparation
Academic user charges At reporting and by term schedule Assessment support, practical materials, and academic services
Boarding-related charges For boarding students only Residential supervision, utilities, and boarding support items
House and welfare charges At reopening or as communicated Student welfare support, house administration, and approved levies
Uniform and starter materials Usually at first admission stage School-branded items and reporting essentials where applicable

Payment guidance

Step Guidance
Before payment Confirm the approved amount and reference details from the school office
During payment Use the approved payment channel and keep all transaction evidence
After payment Present proof of payment for verification and receipt processing where required
If unsure Contact the accounts office before paying to avoid errors or duplicated charges

Important reminders for families

Most fee disputes are avoided when records are kept and questions are asked early.

  • Always keep receipts and transaction confirmations
  • Do not rely on verbal amounts from unofficial sources
  • Ask for clarification if any listed charge is unclear
  • Review reporting instructions so payments align with reopening plans

Accounts office support

The accounts office can explain approved fee components, payment stages, and receipt confirmation for families.

Admissions coordination

If your admission decision is still pending, use the admissions office for status checks before making any payment assumptions.

Planning ahead

Families should connect fee planning with reporting dates, boarding needs, and programme-specific preparation requirements.
