Vision & Mission

Our commitment to excellence, Islamic values, and holistic education shapes every aspect of Nuriya Islamic Senior High School.

Our Vision

To become a center of excellence in Islamic education, producing academically competent and morally upright citizens who contribute meaningfully to national development.

Our Mission

To provide quality secondary education rooted in Islamic principles, fostering intellectual growth, spiritual development, and character building.

Our Core Values

  • Excellence in Education
  • Islamic Morality & Ethics
  • Discipline & Respect
  • Teamwork & Unity
  • Integrity & Accountability
  • Inclusiveness & Diversity

Holistic Development

We nurture students academically, spiritually, and socially, preparing them for leadership and service in their communities.

Global Perspective

Our curriculum encourages critical thinking, innovation, and respect for diversity, equipping students for a changing world.

Community & Service

We instill a sense of responsibility, compassion, and service, inspiring students to make a positive impact wherever they go.
