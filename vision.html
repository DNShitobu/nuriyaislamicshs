Home / Vision & Mission

A school direction built around conviction, clarity, and service

Our vision and mission give direction to teaching, discipline, student welfare, and the way we prepare young people for university, work, and responsible citizenship.

Vision

To be a respected Islamic senior high school known for disciplined scholarship, ethical leadership, and strong preparation for higher education and lifelong service.

Mission

To deliver quality secondary education grounded in faith, purposeful teaching, structured welfare, and a culture that encourages responsibility and academic excellence.

Core values

  • Faithfulness and integrity
  • Discipline and punctuality
  • Respect and service
  • Academic diligence
  • Leadership with humility

The graduate we aim to shape

Students should leave Nuriya with more than examination success. They should also possess habits and attitudes that help them use that success well.

  • Confident in faith and respectful in conduct
  • Able to learn independently and think critically
  • Prepared for tertiary education, work, and civic responsibility
  • Committed to honesty, service, and personal discipline

Strategic pillars

The school's work is organized around a few pillars that keep priorities clear for staff, students, and families.

  • Strong classroom instruction and academic monitoring
  • Reliable supervision, guidance, and student welfare
  • Visible Islamic values in speech, dress, and conduct
  • Parent partnership and community accountability

How the vision shows up in daily school life

Vision matters only if it changes decisions and routines. These are some of the ways it is made visible on campus.

In the classroom

Teachers set high standards, insist on preparation, and use continuous feedback to help students improve rather than drift.

In boarding and welfare

House staff emphasize order, routine, personal responsibility, and the support needed for students to stay focused.

In leadership formation

Students are encouraged to practice service, public speaking, teamwork, and respectful influence through clubs and house activities.
